2024 Interactive Installation Exploring Thalassophobia

Thalassoscope transforms deep-water phobia into an emotional experience. It mimics ocean depth with changing lights and whirling patterns using acrylic, mirrors, motors, ultrasonic sensors, and NeoPixels. Users start the trip with an arcade button, moving from serene surface images to turbulent deep-sea events. The last design focused on replacing servos with gear motors, improving sensory engagement, and boosting structural stability. Thalassoscope, made of sustainable materials and digital technology, is an artistic and engineering feat that evokes curiosity, amazement, and discomfort, like the unknown depths it represents.

2025 Hybrid Unity Game Design – “Wicked Run” with Arduino Motion Controls

Wicked Run, a Unity infinite runner, using Arduino Leonardo and MPU6050 IMU sensors for real-time motion controls. The tilt sensor in a homemade witch hat controls hopping, sliding, and evading to dynamic music. Our team developed music-driven opponent AI, HID joystick support, and serial-to-Unity input processing. Unity version control, collaboration programming, and motion input/game logic were challenges. Our hybrid physical-digital interactive prototype combines rhythm, motion, and immersive narrative into a unique gaming experience using modular scene design, testing, and simulated input debugging.

2025 Thai Art Interactive Installation at the CSM Exhibition—Neighbours, look outside!

Neighbours, two Thai students' interactive digital artwork at Central Saint Martins, looks outdoor! The exhibit explores human-technology connections using TouchDesigner and MediaPipe to combine Thai culture with creative coding. Webcam movements trigger generative scenes—rain particles, undulating drapes, and Thai spirit house sunsets—on old CRT TVs and digital projections. Environment and hardware limits shaped the architecture, but SOP, TOP, and CHOP operators pushed real-time interactivity. The piece celebrates Thai digital art and promotes cross-cultural dialogue through a responsive story.

2025 Coral Bleaching Installation – Offer Holder Day Selection + University Exhibition

Coral bleaching is an ecological and human tragedy in this immersive piece. TouchDesigner and a unique HTML/CSS/JavaScript web interface provide real-time visuals and interactivity. Coral reef devastation from warming oceans and neighborhood-based protection should be studied. Through digital narrative and tactile inquiry, the dual-system arrangement depicts coral's strength and fragility. Coastal communities' pollution avoidance, reef regeneration, and marine zone preservation stories anchor the experience in daily living and promote consideration on climate change's influence on ocean conservation. Our interactive display at Offer Holder Day and the university event exhibition “Refractor” will present our course and explain how we employ creative technologies to address environmental challenges.

2025 Expanded Temporalities – Collaborative Interactive Installation (In Progress)

This joint installation examines animals' temporal perceptions. The project uses TouchDesigner, Unity, and Arduino to create immersive, multi-sensory experiences using physical interaction, projection mapping, and HCI. Participants view projections of a 100-year-old tree and a three-day fly on a revolving cube. A collaborative puzzle game in the exhibit teaches visitors about each species. We want to challenge human-centered time and let people embody non-human perspectives through tales, interactions, and shared experiences by establishing different temporal senses.
